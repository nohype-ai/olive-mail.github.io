Email as Context for Agents
Controlled, Private, Token-Efficient

Existing mail accounts hold crucial context for real work.
Agents need that context. They don't need your passwords, unrestricted right to send, or sensitive information from emails.

Olive Mail puts you in control.
It's a local mediator that you authenticate. Agents never touch credentials. They use Olive Mail to search, read, draft or send.

Olive Mail is not just a security layer.
It also aims to offer granular permission control, fast cached access, semantic search, and custom redaction plugins.

agent-user 
$ olive-mail account add you@work.com --imap imap.example.com
Added account you@work.com. Agents will not touch that password.

$ olive-mail email list --mailbox Inbox
42  Alice Chen  alice@work.com  1 Jun 2026

$ olive-mail email show --mailbox Inbox 42 --fields subject
Subject: Re: Q3 close

$ olive-mail email show --mailbox Inbox 42 --fields body
Denied: You see email meta data, not email content.

Why We Built This

Direct Access, Connectors or Agent Emails Are Not Solutions

Locked out

Typical agents see no real email context – locked into their own little mailbox. The threads that actually matter — clients, legal, the work — stay dark.

All-Powerful

Or they can do it all – holding the keys to your kingdom. One prompt-injected message, and the inbox is public. Agents can speak as you, emailing anyone, destroying anything.

Leaking Sensitive Data

Real emails often contain sensitive data that a business needs to filter or redact before letting agents read emails at all.

Slow and Expensive

Agents accessing mail accounts directly is slow, loads redundant data, offers no meaningful search, and costs a lot of tokens.

Where We're Going

A Security and Context Gateway – Not Another AI Inbox

  • Protected Credentials

    Your credentials are isolated from agents, by actual account separation. You authenticate. They call olive-mail with a scoped interface, not the password file. You monitor and revoke.

  • Granular Permission Control

    By unix account and mail account. Reading permissions by mailbox, email field, sender. Permissions to draft and send, by destination. Rate limits. Whatever it takes.

  • Redaction of Sensitive Data

    Sensitive data — PII, legal, finance — stripped locally by custom plugins, so a remote model never sees what it should not. The mailbox stays the system of record. The agent sees a view.

  • Fast and Cheap Access

    A local cache across agents for super fast responses. Get locally generated summaries. Extract content from HTML. Only pay for (remote) tokens where frontier AI is actually needed.

  • Outbound Authenticity

    Agents can but don't have to act as spokesperson for you or your company. You can employ them like interns, so they are in the loop while you're still the one who speaks.

  • Semantic Search

    Search for meaning with a prompt - not just for matching terms. Retrieve relevant content instead of stuffing the whole inbox into the agent.
